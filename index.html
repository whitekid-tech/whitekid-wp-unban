🔓 WhatsApp Unban & Recovery Guide

No website can unban a number. Only WhatsApp can. This guide shows the official routes. Anyone promising a paid or instant unban is likely a scam.

My account says “banned” or “temporarily banned”
  1. Open WhatsApp and read the message on screen. It tells you if the ban is temporary and for how long.
  2. Tap Request a review (or “Support”) if it appears, and describe the problem calmly.
  3. Wait. Repeated requests can delay the review.
  4. If there is no in-app option, email support with your number in full international format, e.g. +263..., and explain that you use the official app.
Common reasons for bans

Using modified apps (GB WhatsApp, etc.), bulk or automated messages, many people blocking or reporting you, joining many unknown groups, or sending spam links. Uninstall unofficial apps and use the official one.

Emergency: my account was hacked or stolen
  1. Install WhatsApp and sign in with your number to get a new code. This logs the hacker out.
  2. If you see “Enter your two-step PIN”, you must wait up to 7 days or use your reset email.
  3. Warn your contacts and groups, and turn on two-step verification right away.
Why am I seeing a new-device or code SMS I did not ask for?

Someone may be trying your number. Do not share the code with anyone, even a “friend” asking for it.

🎓 WhatsApp Safety Academy

Fake jobs

Real employers do not charge fees, ask for deposits, or hire only through WhatsApp chats. Check the company on its official website.

Fake investments

“Guaranteed” or daily returns, trading signals and “double your money” offers are classic scams. Real investments carry risk.

Phishing & code theft

Never share your 6-digit code or PIN. Do not log in through links sent in chat. WhatsApp never asks for your code in a message.

Turn on security
  1. Settings → Account → Two-step verification → Enable.
  2. Settings → Privacy → set Groups to “My contacts”.
  3. Settings → Linked devices: remove any device you do not know.
